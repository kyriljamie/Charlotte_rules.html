Simple rules for a clean, healthy, and enjoyable stay.

👟

Maintain a clean and healthy environment, especially for kids.

Unsure? Please message us! We're here to help.

Videoke hours

Videoke must stop at 10:00 PM, per CIDG rules. Please keep doors and windows closed and respect neighbors and community peace.

Maintain a tidy cabin for your health and comfort. Optional caretaker cleaning: ₱500.

Wait 5 minutes for inspection. Check your personal belongings and report damages or missing items.
